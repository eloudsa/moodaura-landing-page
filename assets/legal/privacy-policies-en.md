# Privacy Policy for MoodAura

**Last updated:** July 14, 2025

Noratek SRL (“we”, “our”), a company registered in Belgium with VAT number BE0470043093, is committed to protecting the
privacy of users of the MoodAura app (“App”). This policy explains how we handle your data when you use the App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Age Restriction

MoodAura is available to users of all ages. However, we recommend that children under 16 use the App under the
supervision of a parent or guardian. The App is designed to operate **without collecting personal information**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

MoodAura is built to respect your privacy. We do **not** collect or store personal data such as names, email addresses,
or location. Only the following types of data may be used:

- **Usage data and crash reports**
- **Health data, if you explicitly consent** (e.g., heart rate, sleep, step count)

Health data access is **strictly opt-in** and only allowed with your explicit permission. If granted, this data is *
*stored only locally** on your device and is never shared or transmitted. You can withdraw consent at any time in your
device settings.

Your mood entries, health data, comments, and photos remain **exclusively on your device**.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We only use aggregated and anonymous data to:

- Maintain and improve app functionality
- Understand usage trends
- Identify and fix bugs or crashes
- Ensure technical performance and security

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All personal data (moods, comments, photos) is stored **locally on your device**. We do not process or store any of it
externally. Therefore:

- You retain full control over your data
- Uninstalling the app deletes all data permanently
- We implement in-app security measures, but your device’s security is also essential

---

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do **not sell, share, or transmit** your personal information to any third party.

We may share anonymous, aggregated usage statistics with partners or service providers **only to improve the App**. We
may also disclose information when legally required to do so.

---

## 6. Your Data Rights (GDPR)

MoodAura complies with the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). However, due to the App’s *
*local-only data design**:

- We do **not collect or store personal data**
- No login or account creation is required
- No identifiable user profile exists

This means your GDPR rights (such as access, correction, or portability) are entirely in your hands:

- **Access/edit**: View or modify data directly in the App
- **Deletion**: Uninstalling the App deletes all your data
- **No tracking or profiling**: We do not monitor or analyze user behavior

For questions, contact us at [privacy@moodaura.app](mailto:privacy@moodaura.app).

---

## 7. Uninstalling and Data Deletion

You can delete all your data anytime by uninstalling the App. This will permanently erase your moods, comments, and
photos from your device.

---

## 8. Communication

We do not send marketing emails, push notifications, or ads. Any communication from us is limited to support or critical
updates related to privacy or app functionality.

---

## 9. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use third-party tools like **Sentry** to collect anonymous crash and usage reports. These may include:

- Device type
- Operating system version
- App version
- Crash logs

This helps us improve MoodAura’s performance. For details, see [Sentry’s Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

---

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be posted with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App
implies your acceptance of the changes.
