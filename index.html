What MoodAura Offers

A powerful, private tool to help you explore and understand your emotional life.

  • Mood Tracking

    Record your mood, intensity, activities, notes, and even location—multiple times per day.

  • Visual Mood Timeline

    Explore your emotional patterns through a beautiful, interactive timeline and matrix visualization.

  • Smart Journaling

    Reflect more deeply with expressive notes, tags, and search-friendly entries.

  • Analytics & Reports

    Gain insights through data visualizations and generate detailed PDF reports you can share or keep private.

  • Privacy-First Sync

    Offline-first by design. Your data stays local by default with optional encrypted cloud backup via Firebase.

Take a Look Inside

Explore the clean, intuitive interface of MoodAura.

Express with Your Aura

Add and move the aura to match your current mood. Create a visual representation of how you feel with customizable colors and intensities that reflect your emotional state.

Complex Emotions

Add up to 5 moods at once because emotions are rarely simple. Capture the full spectrum of what you're feeling in any given moment.

Add Context

Give some context about your mood with notes, activities, and location. Understanding the "why" behind your emotions helps identify patterns and triggers.

Timeline View

Visualize your moods through a timeline that shows your emotional journey over days, weeks, or months. Spot trends and patterns at a glance.

Export Your Journey

Display or export in PDF a log of your moods. Perfect for sharing with therapists or keeping a personal record of your emotional well-being journey.

Gentle Reminders

Be notified everyday to allow MoodAura to ask you how you feel or following a change of your mood. Never forget to check in with yourself.

Growing Support

Get some help with a section that will grow over time. Access resources, tips, and guidance for better emotional well-being.

Bilingual Support

Use the app in English or French. MoodAura speaks your language to make emotional tracking comfortable and accessible.
